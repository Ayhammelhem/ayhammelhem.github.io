THE EVALUATION DESKDemo

Prompt requirement“Place a red mug to the left of a closed blue notebook on a white desk.”

Hypothetical output: The mug is red and the notebook is blue and closed, but the mug appears to the right. Lighting and image quality are strong.

Objects & attributesPass
Spatial relationshipFail
Visual qualityPass
Read the evaluation rationale +

Decision: requires correction. The explicit left/right requirement is not met. Strong aesthetics do not compensate for a prompt-adherence failure. Move the mug to the notebook's left and reassess the corrected output.

Rubric anchor: Judge left/right from the viewer's perspective unless the prompt specifies otherwise; flag unclear viewpoints for review.
