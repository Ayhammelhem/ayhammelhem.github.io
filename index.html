01 / IMAGE EDIT EVALUATION

One edit. Five checks.

Original illustrative example · AI-generated images

I break an editing request into separate checks, compare the input and output, and explain which requirements were met. Visual appeal is assessed separately from instruction following.

EDIT REQUEST

“Add one small sailboat with a white sail on the water to the right of the lighthouse. Make the sky fully overcast. Keep the coastline, lighthouse, and camera angle unchanged.”

R1
Was exactly one sailboat added?

One sailboat is visible on the water in the edited image.

YES
R2
Does the boat have a white sail?

The boat carries a visible white sail, matching the requested attribute.

YES
R3
Is the boat to the right of the lighthouse?

The boat sits on the water to the viewer’s right of the lighthouse.

YES
R4
Is the sky fully overcast?

The sky remains blue and the scene is sunlit. The requested cloud cover is missing.

NO
R5
Are the surrounding scene and camera angle preserved?

The lighthouse, rocky headland, shoreline, and framing remain consistent with the input.

YES
4 / 5 requirements met

The boat’s count, appearance, and placement meet the request, but the weather change is missing. Request an overcast-sky revision and recheck that the coastline and lighthouse remain intact.

AI ARTIFACTS & PHOTOREALISM REVIEW

Does the image look natural?

Visual judgment: mostly natural at this viewing size. The boat fits the scene’s scale and perspective. No obvious duplicated objects, broken outlines, or impossible geometry stand out in this view.

Geometry & detail

The lighthouse and sailboat have coherent silhouettes. Fine rigging is too small to assess confidently.

Lighting & integration

The bright sail is compatible with the sunny scene, and the hull meets the water plausibly. This still fails the requested overcast weather.

Texture & scene consistency

Rocks, shrubs, and water show varied detail. The saturated blue palette is not, by itself, evidence of AI generation.

Known origin: both images were generated for this demonstration. Visual naturalness is a quality judgment, not proof of whether an image is AI-generated. A closer review of small details may change the rating.

02 / CHATBOT RESPONSE EVALUATION

Useful answers follow the brief.

Original illustrative example

I compare responses for instruction following, relevance, clarity, and safety, then justify the preference with specific evidence.

USER PROMPT

“Give a new NOC analyst exactly three bullet-point checks before escalating a disk-space alert. Keep the whole answer under 45 words. Do not suggest deleting files.”

RESPONSE A · 34 WORDS
  • Confirm the server, affected volume, current usage, and alert threshold.
  • Check whether usage is rising and whether a maintenance activity explains it.
  • Record the findings and contact the responsible team using the escalation procedure.
RESPONSE B · 58 WORDS
  1. Start by checking the server name and reviewing the alert.
  2. Look at disk usage and decide whether it appears unusually high.
  3. Check recent maintenance or deployments and collect relevant screenshots.
  4. Delete old logs and temporary files to free up space before contacting anyone.
  5. If the warning continues, send a detailed message to the team and wait for instructions.
Evaluation criterionResponse AResponse B
Exactly three checksPass · 3Fail · 5
Under 45 wordsPass · 34Fail · 58
Bullet-point formatPassFail · numbered list
No file-deletion advicePassFail · suggests deleting logs
Relevant to NOC escalationFocused checksPartly relevant
Preference: A is much better

A satisfies the requested format and length while covering alert validation, context, and escalation evidence. B adds extra checks, exceeds the word limit, and directly violates the instruction against file deletion.
